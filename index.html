Academic research profile

Dijana Tolic, PhD

AI in Science · Complex Systems · Machine Learning · Physics

PhD in theoretical physics from the University of Zagreb. Postdoctoral research in mathematical machine learning and complex networks at the Ruder Boskovic Institute, followed by a postdoctoral fellowship at ETH Zürich in physics of complex systems and risks.

My research interests include AI for scientific discovery, complex systems, machine learning, multi-agent systems, agent behavior and cognition, physics-inspired neural networks, open-ended agent discovery, and broader social impact of AI.

Recent Research

Machine learning in science

EUCLID-SAGE

A benchmark suite and U-Net model family for strong gravitational lens discovery using real and simulated Euclid images, including UNet-Radius-mini for Einstein radius estimation.

PreprintRepositoryModelsLensPlayground
AI agents in science

SI-Scientist

Self-Improving Agent Teams for Scientific Research. Multi-agent LLM team for algorithm discovery and unsupervised learning. Agents revise their own instructions, iterate, and adapt to improve in scientific tasks.

Repository
AI interpretability

AttentionRSA

Attention to Physics: Interpreting Transfer Learning of the Einstein Radius in Strong Lens Images. We introduce U-Net models with an attention mechanism to study their interpretability with representational similarity analysis (RSA).

Repository
LLM Evaluation · Model Behavior

PRISM-4D

A benchmark and evaluation framework for risk attitudes and moral preferences in LLMs, using twin scenarios, expected-harm regret, dominance violations and counterfactual sensitivity.

Preprint Repository
LLM Reasoning

Higher-Order ToM

Evaluating higher-order theory-of-mind reasoning in LLMs using multi-agent stories and temporal causal belief graphs.
